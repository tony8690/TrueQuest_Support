The short version.

TrueQuest saves drafts and play history on your device and uses Apple’s CloudKit to publish and share hunts. Your invitation gives its holders access to your hunt plan. Progress and help are shared separately with the organiser. TrueQuest includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

1. Who we are

TrueQuest is developed and provided by Tony Ashton. In this policy, “we” means the TrueQuest developer. For privacy questions or requests, email tony-dev86@outlook.com.

This policy covers information handled by TrueQuest. Apple separately operates your Apple Account, iCloud, Maps, payment and notification services under its own terms and privacy notices. Organisers also choose the content they publish and the people they invite.

2. Information the app uses

Hunts you create or download
Titles, descriptions, instructions, stop coordinates, clues, clue photos, quiz questions and answers, route settings and deadlines. Drafts are local; publishing uploads the hunt content to CloudKit.
Your nickname and hunt participation
Your chosen nickname, an app-generated participant identifier, hunt and session identifiers, readiness and play state, and start and completion times. CloudKit also uses Apple-managed account and share participant identities to control access.
Progress and scan records
Found stops, scan timestamps, locally recorded scan coordinates, quiz progress, completion information and any local photo-checkpoint images. Shared progress includes found stop identifiers and times, but does not include your scan coordinates or checkpoint photos.
Help and replies
Help requests, boundary-exit alerts, their timestamps and available location, plus the organiser’s reply text. These are sent through the private channels set up between hunter and organiser.
Optional grown-up contact number
A number entered by the organiser is stored in local settings and hunt data, and in the organiser’s private iCloud records. It is provided to a connected hunter through their private reply channel so they can choose to send a text message.
Purchases and support
The app checks Apple’s purchase entitlement for the unlimited-publishing unlock. Apple handles payment details. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send.

3. Who can see shared information

Hunt invitations are reusable links. Anyone holding a hunt’s invitation can accept read-only access while the hunt is available. They can see the published route, clues, photos, quiz content and settings. Share links only with people you intend to invite, and avoid putting sensitive information in a hunt plan.

Your progress is shared separately. When private setup is completed, the organiser can see your nickname, progress, scan times, session state and help requests. Another hunter is not given access to those private progress records. Replies and the optional grown-up number are shared privately with the relevant hunter.

Apple manages the identities and participant information used by iCloud sharing; a nickname does not make an iCloud share anonymous. Permissions apply to Apple Accounts. People using the same Apple Account do not have separate sharing identities.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising or include advertising trackers. We do not operate a separate TrueQuest server for hunt content.

4. Location, camera and photos

Location: while you use the app, precise location supports stop-distance checks, hot-and-cold guidance, map positioning and hunt boundaries. Coordinates recorded at a successful scan stay in local progress storage and are excluded from uploaded progress.

Help and boundaries: a help request or an automatic boundary-exit alert can send your available precise location to the connected organiser. If you choose the text-message option, Messages opens with a prepared message that can include a map link. You control whether that message is sent.

Camera and photos: camera access is used for QR scanning and photo checkpoints. Creator-selected clue photos are uploaded as part of the shared hunt. Hunter checkpoint photos are stored locally for the completion recap and are not included in shared progress. QR scanning does not upload a camera recording.

Notifications: optional notifications support help alerts and progress updates. Live Activities can show a hunt title and aggregate progress on the lock screen. You can manage notification, Live Activity, camera and location access in your device’s Settings.

5. Why information is used

Information is used to create and run hunts, validate stops, preserve progress, provide the sharing you request, deliver help and replies, verify purchases and answer support enquiries.

Where data protection law applies, we rely on performance of the service you request for core app functions, legitimate interests in providing support and protecting the service, and consent where required for optional processing. You can withdraw consent for optional processing without affecting earlier lawful processing. Device permissions can also be changed at any time; features that need them may then be unavailable.

6. Storage and retention

On your device: drafts, downloaded hunts, progress and checkpoint photos are held in the app’s local storage. Removing a hunt from My Hunts removes its local hunt and progress records; separate photo files may remain in app storage. Removing the app removes its local app container, subject to any device backups you keep.

In iCloud: published hunts and shared records can remain in the owning Apple Account until removed using applicable owner or iCloud controls. TrueQuest does not automatically purge published hunt records when a deadline passes. Deleting a hunt from My Hunts does not delete its CloudKit records.

Closing a hunt: Close Hunt stops new joins once its iCloud update succeeds. It does not erase existing downloaded copies, previous progress or private reply channels.

Help messages: requests and replies older than 24 hours are eligible for cleanup when the owning app performs the relevant online operations. They can remain longer if the app is offline or cleanup fails; this is not a guaranteed 24-hour deletion deadline.

Support correspondence: we keep enquiries for as long as needed to resolve the issue, handle relevant follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. Apple and your email provider apply their own retention practices to their services.

7. Your choices and rights

You choose your nickname, what content you add and who receives invitations. Joining someone else’s hunt requires private progress and reply setup before play. You can manage device permissions in Settings, remove local hunts, and close hunts you organise.

To ask about access, correction, deletion or a copy of information, email tony-dev86@outlook.com. Tell us what you want to manage without sending passwords or a live invitation link. We may need enough information to verify the request and identify the appropriate record owner.

Depending on your location and the legal basis, your rights may include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights have legal limits. We cannot remotely erase copies another person has already downloaded; an organiser or Apple may need to help with records controlled by their account.

You can raise a concern with your local data protection authority. In the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

8. Children and families

TrueQuest can be used for family adventures. An adult should organise and supervise activities, manage sharing and permissions, and help younger hunters understand what is shared. Choose a nickname rather than a full name and avoid including a child’s personal contact details or identifying photos in a shared hunt.

The app does not ask for a date of birth. Apple Account access and family controls are managed by Apple. If you believe a child’s information has been shared inappropriately, contact the organiser and the developer so we can help identify the available controls.

9. Apple services and security

TrueQuest uses Apple CloudKit, Maps, StoreKit and notification services. Apple processes the account, network and service information necessary to provide them. Its services may process information in different countries under Apple’s applicable terms and safeguards. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and iCloud terms for details.

TrueQuest uses CloudKit access controls and read-only shares. Your device lock, Apple Account security and the people you give invitation links to also affect the protection of your data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. This website

This static website does not set cookies, run analytics or load advertising trackers, external fonts or third-party scripts. There is no contact form or website account. Contact links open your own email application.

The website’s hosting provider may process normal request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. If you email us, your email provider and ours also process the correspondence.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version.
