Getting started

What do I need to use TrueQuest?

TrueQuest runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Publishing and joining a hunt require an internet connection and an active iCloud account. Each person should use their own Apple Account for private progress and replies.

Hunters need a device with camera and precise location access to scan QR cards and confirm that they have reached each stop.

How do I create and prepare a hunt?
  1. Choose Create a Hunt, add your instructions and place stops on the map.
  2. Add clues, optional quizzes and any photo checkpoints. Review the route and publish when it is ready.
  3. Print the stop QR cards or share the printable PDF, then place the cards along your route.
  4. Use Test This Hunt to walk the course before inviting hunters.

A published hunt is a fixed version. To change it, use Duplicate as new draft, edit and publish the copy. The new hunt needs its own invitation and freshly printed QR cards.

Inviting & joining

Can everyone use the same invitation?

Yes. Open your published hunt and tap Invite hunters to copy its link, send it or show its invitation QR code. Everyone joining that hunt can use the same reusable invitation. Reopening the screen returns the same link.

The hunt must be published, open and within its deadline for new hunters to join. Each separate hunt has its own invitation. Anyone with the link can join the hunt while it is available, so share it only with your intended group.

Why will an invitation not open?

Check that you are signed in to iCloud and have an internet connection. Open the organiser’s invitation, paste it under Join a Hunt, or use Scan invitation to read its QR code.

Use the complete iCloud sharing link, including anything after a question mark or hash. An old six-character hunt code does not grant access to private sharing. Ask the organiser to copy the link directly from Invite hunters and confirm that the hunt is still open and has not expired.

If the error remains, email support with the exact message. You do not need to send your invitation link.

How do we connect progress and private replies?

Complete these steps together while both devices are online:

  1. The hunter accepts the organiser’s reusable hunt invitation.
  2. On Ready to explore, the hunter opens Share progress with organiser. The organiser scans that setup QR code using Scan a player’s setup code, or pastes its link into Join.
  3. The organiser opens the hunter’s private reply invitation from their dashboard row. The hunter scans or pastes it and checks setup readiness.

This connects the hunter’s progress and help messages with the organiser’s replies. Finish setup before heading somewhere without a connection.

Playing outdoors

Can we play without an internet connection?

Yes, after downloading the hunt and completing setup online. Downloaded clues, QR scanning, stop checks, quizzes and saved progress work offline. Map imagery may still need a connection.

Progress updates and help messages require internet access. Updates can arrive later when a device reconnects and the app can sync. The organiser dashboard shows synced progress, rather than continuous location tracking.

Why will a QR code or location check not work?

Confirm that the card belongs to the current hunt and stop. In the device’s Settings app, allow TrueQuest to use the camera and location While Using the App, with Precise Location enabled.

Move to the actual stop, give GPS a moment to settle and retry the location check. The app needs a recent, accurate position within the organiser’s scan distance. If stops must be found in order, complete the earlier stop first.

Why has progress or a help message not arrived?

Check that private progress and reply setup is complete and both devices are connected. Open TrueQuest on both devices so it can refresh and sync. The hunter’s screen shows whether progress is saved locally, syncing or synced.

For help messages, check the displayed delivery state and use the retry option if needed. Closing the app, losing connectivity or denied notification permission can delay what the organiser sees. Help and boundary alerts are not a replacement for adult supervision.

Purchases & saved hunts

What is free, and is there a subscription?

Creating drafts, duplicating hunts, joining and playing are free. Your first two successful hunt publications are free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited publishing. There is no subscription.

The upgrade screen shows the price for your App Store region. Deleting a published hunt does not return a free publication slot.

How do I restore my purchase or request a refund?

Open TrueQuest’s Settings and choose Restore Purchase. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock and check your internet connection.

Apple handles payments and refund decisions. See Apple’s instructions for requesting a refund.

Can I restore my hunts after reinstalling?

In My Hunts, use the cloud restore button to retrieve hunts published by your current iCloud account. Restoring a purchase is separate from restoring published hunts.

Unpublished drafts, downloaded hunts and local play history are stored on the device. Restoring published hunts does not restore all of that local content.

What happens when I delete or close a hunt?

Deleting a hunt from My Hunts removes its local entry. It does not delete its published iCloud records or disable its invitation.

As the organiser, use Close Hunt to stop new joins once the change reaches iCloud. Closing retains existing progress and reply information and cannot erase copies already downloaded on other devices.

See privacy choices and deletion information for more about managing your data.
