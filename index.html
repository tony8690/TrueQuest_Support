From an idea to an afternoon out

A great day out.
A few simple ingredients.

A park, a trail, a few good hiding places. You bring the imagination. TrueQuest helps you put the pieces together.

  1. 01

    Create your course.

    Choose your stops on a map. Add clues, photos and quizzes, then decide whether hunters follow a route or find their own way.

  2. 02

    Print the trail.

    Print your QR cards, place them at the stops and test the course. Each scan brings your hunters one discovery closer to the finish.

  3. 03

    Invite. Explore. Enjoy.

    Share one reusable invitation for your hunt. Complete setup while connected, then head out to follow the clues together.

Make it yours

Every clue has
a little character.

A riddle by an old oak. A photo to match. A question only the most curious explorer can answer.

  • More than a pin on a map.

    Mix written clues, photos, quizzes and photo checkpoints to make each stop feel different.

  • A little help, or a little mystery.

    Show stop locations on the map, or let hot-and-cold guidance lead the way.

  • A finish worth finding.

    Celebrate with a completion time, earned badges and a recap of the adventure.

Built around your day

More exploring.
Less organising.

Download your hunt and finish setup before you go. Progress saves on the device, so you can pause for a picnic and pick up where you left off.

When connected, organisers can check shared progress and exchange private help messages. Map imagery, progress updates and help messages need an internet connection.

Start with a little adventure.

Create unlimited drafts and publish your first two hunts for free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited publishing. No subscription. Joining and playing are free.

An iCloud account is needed for publishing and joining. Camera and precise location access support QR scanning and stop checks.
